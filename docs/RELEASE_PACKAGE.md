## Full Store Description

Reciro helps you track receipts and understand your spending without manually typing every purchase.

Take a photo of a receipt, choose one from your gallery, or add a PDF. Reciro uses AI to read the store, date, total amount, category, and items. You can review the result, edit anything that looks wrong, and save the receipt to your personal archive.

Reciro organizes spending by month, category, store, and product, so you can quickly see where your money goes.

Key features:

- Scan receipts with the camera
- Choose receipt photos from your gallery
- Add PDF receipts and complete details manually
- AI receipt analysis for store, date, total, category, and items
- Edit item names, quantities, prices, categories, and totals
- Detect duplicate receipts before saving
- Save receipt photos so you can view them later
- View monthly spending and receipt archives
- Track stores, merchants, categories, and products
- Add custom categories when the defaults are not enough
- Add monthly payments such as rent, subscriptions, bills, and insurance
- Export your data and keep backups in your own iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or Files app
- Automatic app language based on the phone language
- Supported languages: English, Turkish, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, and Dutch
- Currency support for EUR, TRY, GBP, and USD, with receipt currency detection

Reciro is built for simple daily use: scan, save, and simplify.

AI can make mistakes. Always review important receipt details before saving.

## Privacy Summary

Reciro stores receipts, spending, income, budgets, monthly payments, and preferences locally on the user's phone.

Receipt photos and PDF files are kept on the device unless the user deletes them or exports data manually.

Reciro does not store user receipt backups on its own servers.

When AI receipt analysis is used, the selected receipt image or PDF may be sent to the Reciro analysis service and AI provider only to extract receipt details such as store, date, total, category, and items.

Reciro does not sell personal data and does not use receipt content for advertising targeting.

Rewarded ads may be shown through Google AdMob. AdMob may process technical advertising data according to Google's policies.

Payments and subscriptions are handled by App Store and Google Play once Premium is connected.

## Terms Summary

Reciro is a personal receipt and expense tracking tool. It is not a bank, accountant, tax advisor, legal advisor, or financial advisor.

AI receipt analysis may be imperfect. Users are responsible for reviewing important amounts, dates, categories, and item details before relying on reports.

Users are responsible for keeping backups of important data.

Subscriptions, cancellations, refunds, and renewals are handled by the relevant app store once Premium is enabled.
