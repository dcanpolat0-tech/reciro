# Reciro Screenshot Guide

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Use clean sample data. Do not show private receipts, real addresses, phone numbers, card details, or real invoices.

## Recommended Screenshots

Capture screenshots from a real iPhone and a real Android phone after the final build. Use English for the first global store listing.

## Screens To Capture

1. Home
   - Show monthly total, receipt count, recent receipts, and the quick add receipt action.
   - Caption: `See your spending clearly`

2. Add Receipt
   - Show the green add receipt button and camera/gallery choices.
   - Caption: `Add a receipt in seconds`

3. AI Review
   - Show the receipt image, store, date, total, category, and editable products.
   - Caption: `Review AI results before saving`

4. Receipt Detail
   - Show a saved receipt with photo and products.
   - Caption: `Keep every receipt in one place`

5. Reports
   - Show category and store breakdown.
   - Caption: `Understand your spending clearly`

6. Monthly Receipts
   - Show month-by-month receipt archive.
   - Caption: `Browse receipts by month`

7. Products
   - Show most purchased products sorted by quantity.
   - Caption: `See what you buy most`

8. Settings
   - Show money and budget, receipt analysis, data controls, privacy/legal, and feedback.
   - Caption: `Control your data and settings`

## Screenshot Rules

- Use English for the first App Store and Google Play screenshots.
- Use a realistic currency, preferably EUR for the first market.
- Use realistic sample stores and products.
- Do not use blurry receipt photos.
- Make sure the bottom menu is not covering important buttons.
- Battery/time bar should look clean.
- Do not show real personal data.
